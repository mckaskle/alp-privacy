Alp privacy policy

Alp collects no data. Your workouts and settings are stored only on your iPhone. The app sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

A backup is a file the app saves only when you ask. It goes only where you send it.

The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Feedback and crash reports you send through TestFlight go to Apple and to us, under Apple's own terms.

Questions about this policy: privacy@thegoodapp.co.
